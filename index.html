RULES

    How to play

  • You play the game by pressing the tiles in the image and it will cycle through all the possible rail parts you can put on it. To complete the game you all the tiles have to have rails on them except the oasis tiles and all the rails have to form a continiuos loop. You can see the leaderboard when you finish the game once
  • Empty Cell: You can place any tile on an empty cell.
  • Bridge: You can only place straight tiles on a bridge.
  • Mountain: You can only place tiles at a 90-degree angle on a mountain.
  • Oasis: You cannot place any tiles on an oasis.
